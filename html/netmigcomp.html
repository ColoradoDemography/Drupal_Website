SDO Net Migration by Age Dashboard

INSTRUCTIONS:

The following page shows net migration totals and rates by age. Select the county from the list below to view the graphs for a specific county or region. Data is based on Census-year comparisons. Migration estimates for intercensal years can be found in the ACS Migration Flows application.

Select up to 4 geographies and up to 4 time periods.

You can hover over specific data points on each graph to view the data for that specific point.
Click on the download icon on the left of each chart to download the chart and the underlying data.

Information describing a typology of counties by net migration patterns is available here: Net Migration Typology 2023

This data visualization can only be viewed on a tablet or desktop.
